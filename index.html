Frequently Asked Questions

Every tour includes breakfast, lunch, all entrance tickets, a professional English-speaking guide, and travel in a fully air-conditioned 26-seater coach. There are no hidden costs.
All tours depart from Pettah Railway Station, Colombo at 6:00 AM sharp. You will be dropped back at the same location by approximately 8:30 PM.
We're currently accepting bookings via WhatsApp and phone. Simply call or message us at 0716966245 to reserve your seat. Payment details will be provided upon booking.
We recommend comfortable walking shoes, sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses, and a refillable water bottle. For Sigiriya, wear clothes suitable for climbing steps. Modest attire is required for temple visits (cover shoulders and knees).
Absolutely! Our tours are perfect for solo travellers, couples, families and small groups. You'll be joining a friendly group of fellow travellers.
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before the tour date. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 50% charge. No-shows are non-refundable.
Our Galle and Kandy tours are suitable for all ages. The Sigiriya tour involves climbing approximately 1,200 steps, so a reasonable level of fitness is recommended. Please contact us if you have specific concerns.
Our guides are fluent in English and Sinhala. We can arrange Tamil-speaking guides on request with prior notice.
Tours operate rain or shine! Sri Lanka's tropical showers are usually brief. We recommend bringing a light rain jacket. In case of extreme weather, we'll contact you to reschedule.
